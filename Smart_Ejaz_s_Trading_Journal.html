Trade calculator

Enter your trade. The lot size updates instantly. Estimates only, never a guarantee, and it does not place trades.

1. Your trade

Advanced (optional)

2. Result

Assumes a USD account and a standard lot (forex 100,000 units; gold 100 oz, so a 1.00 move = 100 USD per lot). Contract sizes vary by broker, so check yours. For pairs with USD as the base (USDJPY, USDCAD, USDCHF) the conversion uses your entry price. Spread, swaps and gaps are not included; real losses can exceed your stop.

P/L = pips × lot × $10 per pip (JPY pairs use 1000 ÷ exit price). Type a P/L override when the real broker figure differs. Gold counts 0.1 as one pip, JPY pairs 0.01, other pairs 0.0001. Return is measured against your starting balance. Fees are subtracted from P/L unless you type a P/L override. For other instruments, type the pip size and the dollar value of one pip per lot.
